# MatchDesk · Changelog

## 3.0.0

[Novidades em português](docs/WHATS_NEW.pt-BR.md) · [What’s new in English](docs/WHATS_NEW.en-US.md) · [Downloads](DOWNLOADS.md)

### MatchDesk Pro

- My Squad with per-card goals, assists, appearances and goal contributions per appearance, plus separate Steam and Xbox PC squads.
- Controller tester for buttons, sticks, triggers, vibration and polling rate.
- USB polling-rate profiles for compatible wired controllers, with measured rate and restore options.
- PC optimization profiles and explained individual settings, results and recovery.
- Stat-based filtering through integrated Scout using division, ranking, rating and team strength when available.
- Refreshed interface, customizable overlay backgrounds and transparency.

### MatchDesk Scout

- Refreshed application and redesigned opponent overlay.
- Improved platform identification, including Xbox PC.
- Integrated update flow that preserves activation and preferences.

### MatchDesk Mobile

- Green visual identity and ping panel borders that reflect LAN, P2P and server connections.
- Improved reconnection and recovery of monitoring and filters.
- Android updates retain the existing app identity and activation flow.
